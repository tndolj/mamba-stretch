No brace — trust the work. Cut the session short if pain climbs past 3/10.

Strength

Same lifts as Monday. Progress one thing every 1–2 weeks: tempo, reps, step height, or load.
Split squats join here in Phase 2 — shallow first, ATG depth last.

Active rest

Daily base only. Walk, stretch, recover.
Morning knee check: stiffer than yesterday? Yesterday was too much.
Fri

Strength

Same lifts. Keep it 25–30 min, quality over count.
Done early enough that legs are fresh for Saturday.
Sat

Basketball

Daily base first as warm-up. Second and last play day of the week.
Never back-to-back play days in this phase.
Sun

Full rest

Nothing. Tendons adapt while you recover.
Optional: daily base if it feels good — skip the hold.

House rules

1Pain may reach 3/10 during activity, but must settle by the next morning. More than that → next week goes lighter.
2No consecutive basketball days. Consecutive play is what flared this up.
3Progress by pain response, not the calendar. Weeks 1–3+ are isometric; deep loading comes later.
4Run changes past your physio — she's seen your knees move. Ask: "when do I add slow heavy loading, and in what order?"
Better at week 4 ≠ healed. Stay the course.
